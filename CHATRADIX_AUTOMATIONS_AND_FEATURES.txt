PART 1: THE 12 STORE AUTOMATIONS & FUNCTIONALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ORDER CONFIRMATION
   -------------------
   • Trigger: Fires immediately (or after a configured delay) when a customer
     places an order on your Shopify store.
   • Functionality:
     - Sends an order summary with customer name, order number, and total price.
     - Supports 2-way interactive Quick Reply buttons ("Confirm order" / "Cancel order").
     - Automatically updates Shopify order tags (e.g., adds "✅ order-confirmed" and
       removes "⏳ Order-Confirmation-Pending" when the customer taps Confirm).
     - Instantly delivers automated follow-up messages based on the customer's choice.
     - Includes Duplicate Order Protection (flags or suppresses duplicate orders placed
       within customizable time windows like 1h, 6h, 12h, 24h, or same calendar day).
     - Filterable by payment status (all, paid only, or pending/COD).
     - Excludes specific phone numbers or fraud-risk accounts.

2. ABANDONED CHECKOUT RECOVERY
   ---------------------------
   • Trigger: Fires when a shopper enters their phone number in checkout but abandons
     without completing the purchase.
   • Functionality:
     - Multi-step recovery sequence (e.g., Step 1 at 30 mins, Step 2 at 24 hours,
       Step 3 at 48 hours).
     - Employs dynamic tracked recovery links (/r/:token) that restore the customer's
       exact cart, items, and checkout session upon clicking.
     - Automatically ceases subsequent follow-ups the moment the order is completed.
     - Includes single-click "Unsubscribe" / opt-out quick reply for Meta compliance.

3. ORDER FULFILLMENT (SHIPPING UPDATES)
   -----------------------------------
   • Trigger: Triggers automatically when a fulfillment is created or updated in Shopify.
   • Functionality:
     - Notifies the customer that their package is packed and on the way.
     - Dynamically sends carrier name, tracking numbers, and live tracking URLs.
     - Features an interactive "Track Shipment" button that directs customers to the
       carrier portal or your branded store tracking page.

4. ORDER DELIVERED
   ----------------
   • Trigger: Triggered when carrier tracking reports the order status as "Delivered"
     in Shopify.
   • Functionality:
     - Confirms package drop-off to give peace of mind and minimize delivery disputes.
     - Invites the customer to unbox and inspect their items.
     - Sets the stage for feedback and repeat purchases while excitement is highest.

5. ORDER CANCELLATION
   ------------------
   • Trigger: Fires when an order is cancelled or voided in Shopify.
   • Functionality:
     - Delivers immediate reassurance and transparency to the buyer.
     - Confirms cancellation status and outlines refund or credit timelines.
     - Reduces customer support inquiries and chargeback risks.

6. ORDER NOTIFICATION
   ------------------
   • Trigger: Universal lifecycle update for important order events or modifications.
   • Functionality:
     - Sends tailored transaction notices, order edits, or specialized customer alerts.
     - Flexible variable mapping for order items, address updates, and fulfillment details.

7. DRAFT ORDER RECOVERY
   --------------------
   • Trigger: Initiated for B2B, wholesale, custom quote, or manual phone-order drafts
     created in Shopify that remain unpaid.
   • Functionality:
     - Multi-sequence automated reminders sent to wholesale or invoice buyers.
     - Delivers secure, tracked invoice payment links directly to the client on WhatsApp.
     - Halts follow-ups as soon as the invoice is paid and converted into a live order.

8. PRODUCT REVIEW REQUEST
   ----------------------
   • Trigger: Fires N days after an order achieves "Delivered" status in Shopify.
   • Functionality:
     - Verifies actual delivery before counting down (avoiding asking for reviews
       on packages still in transit).
     - Prompts satisfied buyers to leave reviews on your store or product pages.
     - Drives social proof and user-generated content organically.

9. PRODUCT SPOTLIGHT CAMPAIGN (MEDIA CAROUSEL)
   -------------------------------------------
   • Trigger: On-demand or scheduled broadcast campaigns for catalog showcases.
   • Functionality:
     - Sends interactive multi-card WhatsApp media carousels (up to 10 visual cards).
     - Each card displays a product image, title, price, and dedicated "Buy Now" button.
     - Customers swipe horizontally on WhatsApp to browse your catalog and tap to purchase.

10. CUSTOMER CAMPAIGN (SEGMENTED RE-ENGAGEMENT)
    -------------------------------------------
    • Trigger: Scheduled bulk campaigns tailored to customer lists and loyalty tiers.
    • Functionality:
      - Targets customer tiers (e.g., Regular, Loyal, Platinum, VIP).
      - Filterable by minimum customer spend thresholds.
      - Personalizes greetings with first names, order history, and exclusive discount codes.

11. WIN-BACK INACTIVE CUSTOMERS
    ---------------------------
    • Trigger: Background intelligence scanner that identifies customers who have not
      purchased within a set inactivity window (e.g., 60, 90, or 120 days).
    • Functionality:
      - Automatically delivers re-engagement offers and discount vouchers to lapsed buyers.
      - Built-in cooldown periods (e.g., 90 days) prevent message fatigue and spamming.
      - Minimum spend filters ensure offers target historically profitable customers.
      - Dynamically pulls previous order date, last order name, and days since last purchase.

12. ADMIN / STAFF NOTIFICATION
    --------------------------
    • Trigger: Immediate alert sent upon every new order placement.
    • Functionality:
      - Sends high-priority WhatsApp notifications directly to store owners, managers,
        or warehouse staff phone numbers.
      - Contains order ID, customer name, total amount, payment method (COD vs Prepaid),
        and shipping city for rapid fulfillment preparation.

PART 2: MAIN FEATURES & CAPABILITIES OF CHATRADIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   - Direct, high-speed connection through Meta's Official Cloud API.
   - Embedded Signup allows merchants to connect their WhatsApp Business in under 60 seconds.
   - Supports both Coexistence Mode (use WhatsApp Business App and API simultaneously)
     and full Cloud API mode.
   - Self-healing WABA ID discovery automatically validates and auto-subscribes webhooks.

2. FULL-FEATURED TEMPLATE BUILDER & TEMPLATE SHOP
   - In-app template creator supporting Single message and Carousel layouts.
   - Rich headers (Text, Image, Video, Documents) with sample uploads for Meta approval.
   - Dynamic Shopify variables for customers, orders, fulfillments, and line items.
   - Interactive Buttons: URL buttons, Phone Number buttons, Copy Offer Code buttons,
     and Quick Reply buttons.
   - Pre-built "Template Shop" offering pre-approved, high-converting eCommerce templates.

3. TWO-WAY INTERACTIVE REPLIES & SHOPIFY ORDER TAGGING
   - Seamless two-way conversational automation.
   - Quick reply actions on WhatsApp automatically transition Shopify order tags:
     • "Confirm order" -> adds "✅ order-confirmed", removes "⏳ Order-Confirmation-Pending"
     • "Cancel order" -> adds "❌ order-cancelled", removes pending tags
   - Automatically sends instant custom follow-up replies on WhatsApp.

4. 24/7 INBOUND AUTO REPLIER
   - Automated intelligent responder for incoming WhatsApp messages from customers.
   - Define keyword triggers (e.g., "track", "help", "order status", "return") with
     automated answers to resolve customer questions 24/7 without manual staff overhead.

5. SMART TRACKED SHORT LINKS (/r/:token)
   - Built-in URL redirect engine compliant with Meta's strict dynamic URL policies.
   - Restores Shopify abandoned checkouts and draft orders with full tracking.
   - Tracks click-throughs and attributes recovered sales directly in analytics.

6. 1-CLICK MANUAL WHATSAPP MESSAGING FROM SHOPIFY ADMIN
   - Integrated Shopify Admin Action Extensions.
   - Send manual order confirmations, fulfillment updates, or checkout recovery links
     directly from Shopify's native Orders and Draft Orders admin screens with 1 click.

7. COMPREHENSIVE ANTI-SPAM & RELIABILITY SHIELD
   - Duplicate Order Detection: Prevents duplicate messages or flags spam checkout floods.
   - Non-WhatsApp Recipient Detection: Identifies unregistered numbers and tags Shopify
     orders (e.g., "WhatsApp Not Registered") so fulfillment teams can use SMS or phone calls.
   - Payment Issue Safeguard: Flags Meta billing issues with descriptive order tags.
   - Automatic Unsubscribe Processing: Instantly respects customer opt-out requests.

8. STOREFRONT WHATSAPP LIVE CHAT WIDGET
   - Customizable floating WhatsApp chat widget for your Shopify storefront.
   - Configure widget position (bottom-right / bottom-left), branding colors, greeting text,
     and pre-filled message prompts so visitors can initiate presale chats with 1 tap.

9. REAL-TIME ANALYTICS DASHBOARD
   - Live visual charts tracking message volume, delivery rates, and read rates.
   - Revenue attribution tracking total sales and recovered revenue driven by WhatsApp.
   - Automation performance breakdown ranking which automations generate the highest ROI.
